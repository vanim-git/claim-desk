Project Sankalp Pilot

One place to keep Pfizer's channel in check. It checks expiry and breakage claims against policy CO-POL-014 before a credit note is raised, warns when stock at a C&F is heading under 60% remaining shelf life, flags stockists who look over-stocked before a quarter-end scheme, shows unfilled orders and proposes where spare stock could move, and gives each owner a weekly digest. Every number is worked out from the data files and shows its source rows. The app only recommends or proposes; a person decides, and the steps they take are kept in an append-only log.

Which scheme SKUs come back as expiry claims, and which stockists look over-stocked before a scheme. Warns only; the scheme desk decides.

Unfilled orders, allocation shares against what each C&F's stockists order, and spare stock that could move through the CDC. Proposals only: Kavita Rao approves.

One short section per owner, put together from the Expiry watch, Scheme desk and Supply desk results. Read-only: nothing is stored or sent. Each line links to the desk behind it.

Record a step

Append-only: a step is added, never edited or removed. The tool never takes the step; a person did, and types their name here (no sign-in yet).

Facts come from the data files. The model, if switched on, only reads emails and words the explanation. Decisions are written to the log only.
